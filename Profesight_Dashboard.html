How Profesight works

From a quick scan to a clear next step in four moves.

1

Choose a term and section

Filter by academic year, semester, subject, and section. Everything on the page updates to match.

2

Scan the alerts and KPI cards

Alerts list class-level issues. Each of the five cards shows a value, its target, and a status.

3

Open the details

Click a KPI card, a chart bar, or a risk row to compare by KPI or inspect one section in the Details panel.

4

Act early

Use the Section risk estimate to plan a review session or tutoring before grades are final.

Early warning

See which sections may need help before the semester ends

Section risk estimates the chance that each of your sections finishes below 75% passing, using assessment results so far, the previous semester's passing rate and SETE ratings, and class size.

Reads the signals

Assessment average so far, previous passing rate, SETE composite, and class size.

Estimates the risk

Each section is labeled Low, Moderate, or High with a percentage.

Suggests a next step

For example, schedule a review session or refer students for tutoring.

A support signal, not an evaluation of the teacher. SETE is only used with 5 or more respondents, and any decision needs human review.
